## Universal Framework

Every outreach follows this structure:

```
1. HOOK — Reference something specific about their business (review, location, service)
2. PAIN — Name the problem they live with but haven't solved
3. PROOF — Brief, concrete result (not a feature dump)
4. CTA — One simple ask (not "buy now" — just a conversation)
```

### The Golden Rule of Cold Outbound
> **Never lead with what you are. Lead with what they're losing.**

Bad: "We're an AI phone answering service for small businesses."
Good: "When your receptionist is with a patient, what happens to the next call?"

## Objection Handling

### "We already have a receptionist"

**Response:**
> "That's great — I'm not trying to replace them. Think of Synthline as the backup for when they're busy, on lunch, or after hours. You know those gaps where calls still slip through? That's what we fill."

### "We use [Vagaro/Calendly/online booking]"

**Response:**
> "Nice, that handles the online traffic. But what about the people who still call? Studies show 60% of customers prefer calling over booking online — especially for first visits. Synthline catches those callers and books them right into the same system."

### "We're too small / don't get that many calls"

**Response:**
> "Even better — that means every single call matters more. If you miss 3 calls a week and even one of those is a new customer worth $500+, that's $2K/month you're leaving on the table. Synthline costs a fraction of that."

### "AI sounds impersonal / my clients expect a person"

**Response:**
> "Totally fair. Our AI sounds natural — most callers can't tell. And the goal isn't to replace human connection — it's to make sure the call gets answered in the first place. A missed call is zero connection."

### "How much does it cost?"

**Response:**
> "We have a free tier so you can try it with zero risk — 50 calls per month, no credit card. The Professional plan is $299/month for unlimited calls. Most businesses recover the cost in the first week from jobs they would have lost."

### "I need to think about it"

**Response:**
> "Absolutely, take your time. Can I send you a 2-minute video that shows exactly how it works? That way you can share it with your team too. And I'll follow up next [Tuesday] to see what you thought."

### "We already have a voicemail / answering service"

**Response:**
> "How many callers actually leave a voicemail? The industry average is only 15%. The other 85% just hang up and call the next business. Synthline answers every call and actually books the appointment — it's active, not passive."

### "I don't trust AI with my customers"

**Response:**
> "I get that — you care about your customers, that's why you're successful. Think of it this way: right now, 40% of your callers hit voicemail and never come back. They're not getting a human either way. Synthline at least gets them booked. You can always talk to them when they arrive."

### "Can I try before I buy?"

**Response:**
> "That's actually exactly how we work. Free tier — 50 calls/month, no credit card, cancel anytime. You'll see real calls come in within the first week. If it doesn't prove itself, you've lost nothing."

### "I'm in a contract with [competitor]"

**Response:**
> "No problem — when does that contract end? I can set a reminder to reach out a month before so you can compare. In the meantime, want me to send you a cost comparison so you can see how it stacks up?"
