Everything you need to land your first $299/mo customer.

## 📋 DAILY WORKFLOW (2 hours/day)

```
Morning (30 min)
  ☐ Open Google Maps links → find 10 new businesses
  ☐ Copy name, phone, website into tracker

Mid-day (45 min)  
  ☐ Send 30 cold emails (paste from templates)
  ☐ Log each send in tracker

Afternoon (30 min)
  ☐ Call 10 businesses from your list
  ☐ Log outcome in tracker

Evening (15 min)
  ☐ Follow up with previous day's prospects
  ☐ Post 1 LinkedIn update
```

**Targets:**
- 150 emails/week → 5-15 replies → 3-5 demos → 1-2 deals
- 50 calls/week → 8-15 conversations → 2-3 demos → 1 deal

## 📞 WHAT TO SAY ON THE PHONE

**Opening (10 words or less):**
```
"Hi [Name] — quick question about your missed calls?"
```

**If they bite:**
```
"Most [plumbers/dentists/salons] miss 30-40% of calls 
during busy hours. We built an AI agent that picks up 
every single one and books the appointment for you.

Sound like something that could help? Happy to show 
you in 10 minutes."
```

**If they brush you off:**
```
"No worries! Can I send you a 2-min video? If it's 
not for you, I won't follow up."
```
